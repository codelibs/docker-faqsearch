How do I enable two-factor authentication?

To enable two-factor authentication, go to Settings, then Security, then Two-Factor Authentication and start the setup wizard. Scan the QR code shown on screen with your authenticator app, which will generate a six-digit verification code. Enter that code to finish setup; every login will then require a fresh verification code. Store your backup codes somewhere safe in case you lose your device.
